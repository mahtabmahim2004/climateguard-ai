RESPONSIBLE SCIENCE

Limitations & appropriate use

Read before interpreting outputs
  • Rainfall-derived proxy target. The label is positive when the 7-day rainfall total is at least 60 mm and same-day rainfall is at least 10 mm; it is not observed flood ground truth.
  • No observed flood labels. BWDB water levels are not flood labels and do not establish an official threshold.
  • Limited BWDB coverage. One station has a limited 31-day snapshot; external-source historical verification remains limited.
  • No NASA/BWDB overlap. The sources are kept separate; no fused supervised dataset exists.
  • Proxy probability only. A classifier probability refers to its constructed proxy target, not real-world flood probability.
  • Calibration unverified. Persisted calibration metrics for the saved project models are absent.
  • Scenario response only. Perturbations are model stress tests, not physical flood simulations, climate projections, or forecast evaluations.
  • Perfect tree scores are not flood skill. The target is built from rainfall features, so perfect scores show reproduction of a constructed rainfall rule.
  • Not operational infrastructure. This local dashboard is a portfolio/research demonstration, not an official warning service.
